Skyward Sword controls

The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword (Wii, USA) on a normal controller in this RetroArch setup. Button names are Nintendo Pro Controller labels; on an Xbox pad, A and B are the same and X and Y are swapped.

The game thinks it's using a Wii Remote with MotionPlus and a Nunchuk. The right stick stands in for the Wii Remote: a quick flick to the edge is a sword swing; smaller pushes move the pointer, which stays where you leave it.

If something goes wrong

  • Pointer drifts or sits off-centre: press D-pad down, or call Fi. On the “Point the Wii Remote at the screen” screen, move the hand to the middle and press A.
  • Pointer range: the pointer is set to 38° wide by 30° tall (Wiimote IR Total Yaw/Pitch). Don't raise these: past that, the emulated Wii Remote camera loses the sensor bar and the cursor disappears.
  • Stray stabs or shield bashes: lower Wiimote Swing > Swing Speed in the core options (Quick Menu > Core Options).
  • Swings too hard to trigger, or happening while aiming: change Swing Dead Zone (higher = needs a bigger flick).
  • All of these are set only for Skyward Sword; other games are unaffected.
